09 The Location

Find your way
to Mogri.

Gayatri Mandir sits at the heart of Mogri, a short drive from Anand city — about twenty minutes from Anand Junction and under an hour from Vadodara. Every ceremony happens in and around the temple courtyard.

01

By air

Vadodara Airport (BDQ) is about forty-five minutes away; Ahmedabad (AMD) is roughly an hour and forty minutes. Tell us your arrival in the reply and we will help with transfers.

02

By rail

Anand Junction is twenty minutes by road. Autos and taxis wait outside the station — send us your train details and someone will meet you.

03

By road

Mogri sits just off the Anand–Vasad road near NH-48, easy to reach from Vadodara, Ahmedabad or Surat. Parking runs along the temple lane, with volunteers to guide you.
